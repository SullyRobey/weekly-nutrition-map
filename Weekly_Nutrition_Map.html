Reading the week

  • Every day lands within about 15 calories of 2,200, well under your training intake, so weight stays steady to slowly drifting down while you recover.
  • Protein stays high at roughly 175 to 235 g a day to protect muscle while you can't train (Saturday is the lowest). Fat sits at about 45 to 65 g.
  • Calcium reaches about 920 to 1,120 mg a day, helped by skim milk and yoghurt, to support bone and tissue healing.
  • Tuna is on Monday, Wednesday and Friday only. Selenium on those days is 350 to 375 µg, close to the 400 µg upper limit.
  • Sodium stays under 2,000 mg (Monday and Wednesday are highest, near 1,950). Potassium is above 5,000 mg every day, well over your 3,800 mg goal. Vitamin D runs low on Thursday and Sunday.

Figures are estimates from standard food values for the portions in the plan. Vegetables marked "veg" are counted as about 100 g of broccoli. Check product labels and confirm targets with your club dietitian or physio. Vitamin A comes mostly from sweet potato as beta-carotene.
